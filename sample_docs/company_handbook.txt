EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — ACME ROBOTICS INC.

SECTION 1: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval.
Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 AM to
4:00 PM in their local time zone. Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a
secure home network and must use company-issued VPN software when accessing internal
systems.

SECTION 2: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Employees can submit expense reports through the internal Concur portal within 30 days
of the purchase date. Reimbursable expenses include client meals (up to $75 per person),
travel booked through the corporate travel portal, and approved software subscriptions
under $200/month. Personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, increasing to 20
days after three years of tenure. PTO requests must be submitted at least five business
days in advance for planned absences. Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not
require advance notice.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews occur twice a year, in June and December. Reviews are based on a
combination of self-assessment, manager evaluation, and peer feedback. Promotion cycles
are aligned with the December review period.

SECTION 5: EQUIPMENT POLICY
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals within their first
week. Equipment requests outside the standard kit (e.g., additional monitors, ergonomic
chairs) require manager approval and are processed through the IT service desk.
