PRODUCT FAQ — ACME CLOUD PLATFORM

Q: What is the uptime SLA for the Acme Cloud Platform?
A: We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all Enterprise and Business tier customers, measured
monthly. Customers on the Free tier do not have an SLA. If uptime falls below the
guaranteed threshold, Enterprise customers are eligible for service credits as outlined
in the Master Service Agreement.

Q: How is data encrypted?
A: All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Encryption
keys are managed through our internal key management service and rotated every 90 days.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. All plans support data export in JSON or CSV format through the account
settings page. Enterprise customers can also request a full data export via API using
the /v2/export endpoint, which returns a signed download URL valid for 24 hours.

Q: What happens if I exceed my plan's API rate limit?
A: Requests beyond your plan's rate limit will receive a 429 status code. Free tier
accounts are limited to 100 requests per minute, Business tier to 1,000 requests per
minute, and Enterprise tier to 10,000 requests per minute with the option to purchase
additional capacity.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Subscriptions can be canceled anytime from Account Settings > Billing. Cancellations
take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and no partial refunds are issued
for unused time within a cycle.
